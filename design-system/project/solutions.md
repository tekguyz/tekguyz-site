# Solutions and process

The four Solutions are the real offer. Each holds many offerings. A client buys a package: several offerings wired together to fix one problem end to end. A single offering on its own rarely sells. A website is one offering, not the offer.

## The four Solutions

The accent is the Solution's identity everywhere it appears: dot, tag, pull-quote rule, eyebrow. The order never changes: blue, violet, amber, teal.

| Solution | Accent | Tokens | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smart Operations | Blue `#3B6FE0` | `tg-accent-blue`, `tg-accent-blue-on-bg` | Your business makes data and tasks every hour. We build systems that handle them automatically. |
| AI Voice Agents | Violet `#7C6FE0` | `tg-accent-violet`, `tg-accent-violet-on-bg` | Your phones don't stop ringing because your doors are closed. We answer them. |
| Business Systems | Amber `#F2A93C` | `tg-accent-amber`, `tg-accent-amber-on-bg` | Everything your clients and team need, in one private place instead of five. |
| Custom Web Apps | Teal `#2FA679` | `tg-accent-teal`, `tg-accent-teal-on-bg` | If you can describe the workflow, we can build the tool that runs it. |

- Tag labels are the name in capitals: SMART OPERATIONS, AI VOICE AGENTS, BUSINESS SYSTEMS, CUSTOM WEB APPS.
- A build is tagged with the Solution it proves. Never tag it with two.
- No prices, pilots or cadence appear in public copy.
- The CRM is the founder's own tool, offered to a business that needs one. It is not a product sold on its own.

## How we work

Four steps. Keep the names and the order. They are the only numbered sequence in the system, so they are the only place numerals are set large.

1. **Discovery.** Learn the business first: the workflow, the pain points, what success looks like. Remote, or on site.
2. **Blueprint.** Map what gets built and why, what it costs and how long it takes, in plain language. The low-hanging fruit is picked here.
3. **Build.** Regular check-ins. The client sees it working before it is done.
4. **Launch & Support.** We do not disappear after go-live. One-time work ends with the documentation; monthly work keeps going.

Then land and expand: do one job well, learn how the business runs, offer the next fix.

## Who we serve

Small and medium businesses in South Florida first, then the rest of Florida's large cities, then large U.S. metros. Two groups first: home-service trades (HVAC and AC repair, roofing, boat and marine repair and detailing) and independent practices that need more than a website (dental, veterinary). Never enterprise.

## Proof

Every build shown is running, and a visitor can open it. The site checks each one every hour and prints the real result with its time. A demo may run on sample data and carry a demo label; that is by design. Two rules still hold: a picture of a build is a real capture of the running app, and a figure from sample data is never quoted as a result.
