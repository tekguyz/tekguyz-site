One step of the four-step process, with its numeral behind the title.

## What the consumer provides

`numeral`, `title`, the body as children, and `solution` to pick the numeral's accent. Steps go blue, violet, amber, teal in order.

## Rules

- These are the only numerals on the site, because the process is the only ordered sequence.
- The numeral is decoration at 8% opacity, and `aria-hidden`.
- The four steps are Discovery, Blueprint, Build, Launch & Support. Keep them.
