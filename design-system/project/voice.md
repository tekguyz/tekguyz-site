# Voice and words

How TEKGUYZ sounds in anything a client, lead or visitor reads. Read it before writing a headline, a caption, an email or a deck.

## The message, in order

TEKGUYZ is the real person who helps small and medium businesses use real AI to fix real problems. We find the problem first, remote or on site. We pick the low-hanging fruit: the fix with the most impact for the least work. We build it, show it working and document it. Then we find the next one. The work can be one-time or monthly.

What sets TEKGUYZ apart, in this order. Lead with the first; live proof supports the second, it is not the reason to call.

1. A real person who replies, in plain English, and shows the work all the time.
2. Real AI that fixes real problems. Not slop, not hype.
3. Low-hanging fruit first. A small, low-friction start.
4. Live, working proof. Every build we show is running and a visitor can open it.
5. Good documentation. The client knows what was built and what to do when something changes.

## Who is speaking

- **Public copy says "we".** "We" is the founder working with AI agents. It never claims hired staff: no "our engineers", no "our team", no "our staff".
- **A one-to-one message says "I"** and signs with a first name: a DM, a cold email, a reply.
- **The customer is a small or medium business.** Owner-run, not part of a chain, never an enterprise. Say "your business", "your phones", "your clients".

## Rules for every sentence

- Short. One idea. Concrete over abstract.
- Show, do not claim. Link the live build instead of saying "powerful".
- Parallel items stay parallel: the four Solutions, the builds on the work page. Keep each entry about the same length. An outlier reads as a bug.
- Match the reader's language. A Spanish-first business gets Spanish.
- **Never say a thing is built, running or proven when it is not.** No made-up numbers, results, prices or timelines. Where the real fact is missing, ship `[NEEDS COPY: <slot>]`.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji. No engineering words in anything a client reads.

## Do and do not

- Do write: "Your phones don't stop ringing because your doors are closed. We answer them."
- Do not write: "Our expert engineers deliver cutting-edge AI infrastructure."
- Do write "Live · checked 4 minutes ago" only when a real check happened.
- Do not write a rating, a star count or a score. None exists.
- Do put a customer's own words in quotation marks with a source a reader can check: a first name plus "Verified Google review".
- The site's AI concierge is called AI, and the site says so. Never call it a person, and never "Ask a builder". It never states a price or a timeline.
