Three new sites, built from zero

Each idea has its own pages, its own flow, new words and its own motion. Only your facts, your builds, the colors and the wordmark carry over. Open each one on your phone and scroll all the way down.

1 · The Day My pick

Open

The home page is one owner's workday as a scroll story. Each hour is a real problem and the fix acting itself out. The light changes as the day goes on, and the stripe's four colors become the hours.

PagesThe Day (home)One page per fixTry the buildsTalk
Motion
A clock that runs as you scroll. Each fix plays out on a little screen.
Contact
The day ends at night with a note: "What ate your day?"
Risk
The owner has to scroll to reach the contact note. A "Talk to us" button stays at the top.
