Drafted 2026-11-18 from `tekguyz-one/projects/tekguyz-site/about.md`, which wins if the two disagree.

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of small and medium businesses, mostly in South Florida, weighing software, AI or automation help. Most do not know what AI is or how it could fix their problems; they want to see real work before they get in touch. Second user: the founder, who gets every enquiry in the lead CRM.

## Product Purpose

tekguyz.com, the front door of TEKGUYZ. It says in plain words what TEKGUYZ builds, shows the work as case studies with demos, and takes an enquiry. Success: a visitor knows what TEKGUYZ does, believes the work is real, and knows how to start.

## Positioning

Every case study has a demo the visitor can try, and every enquiry goes straight into the CRM, so nobody retypes it and nothing gets lost.

## Operating Context

Four Solution pages (Smart Operations, AI Voice Agents, Business Systems, Custom Web Apps), a Work page with a page per project, a process page, a contact page with an FAQ and a form, a calendar page for a free first conversation, and an AI concierge that answers from the site's own facts. Light and dark themes; works on a phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The concierge never states or estimates a price and never promises a timeline or a time.
- Every key stays on the server.

## Brand Commitments

- TEKGUYZ brand: `tekguyz-one/brand/`. The site's current look lives in its own `design-system/` folder and code (its fonts include Archivo and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next); keep it.
- Never parallax, marquees, particles, glass effects or cursor followers.
- "We" is TEKGUYZ; never "our team" or "our engineers". Say "South Florida" for place.
- This repo has no `DESIGN.md` yet: capture the current look from `design-system/` with `document` before changing it; do not roll a new visual world unless the founder asks for a redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

The shipped demos and their case studies (`tekguyz-one/projects/`). Never invent a metric, a client name or a price.

## Product Principles

- Plain words over jargon; explain AI by what it fixes.
- Show the work, then ask for the enquiry.
- Every way in (form, calendar, concierge) ends in the CRM.
